About Velora

Velora Jewells curates expressive jewellery for personal style — gothic-inspired pendants, chains, bracelets and statement pieces. Product details, prices and available stock shown on a live product page are the source of truth for your order.

Contact & customer support

For an order question, keep your order number and payment reference ready. Email velora1906@gmail.com or WhatsApp 8986581105 for support. We will never ask you to share an OTP, card PIN, UPI PIN or account password.

Please do not send payment details or sensitive identity documents over social media. If an online payment is deducted but an order confirmation is not shown, keep the payment ID and contact support before retrying.

Shipping policy

  • Free shipping is offered across India on eligible store orders; any exception will be shown before payment.
  • Delivery estimates depend on the destination, courier service, weather and operational conditions. An exact delivery date is not guaranteed unless explicitly confirmed.
  • Once an order is dispatched, tracking details will be shared when available from the courier.
  • Please enter a complete delivery address, working mobile number and correct six-digit PIN code at checkout. Incorrect details can delay delivery.
  • If a parcel appears damaged, photograph the outer package before opening it and record the full unboxing.

No Returns · No Exchanges · Refunds Only

IMPORTANT — UNBOXING VIDEO IS MANDATORY FOR REFUND CLAIMS. Velora Jewells does not accept returns or exchanges. Refund requests are considered only for eligible cases such as an item arriving damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered, and must be supported by a continuous, unedited unboxing video.
  • Record one continuous video before opening the parcel. It should clearly show the sealed package, shipping label, opening of the parcel and the item received. Do not pause, cut or edit the recording.
  • Contact support as soon as possible after delivery with your order number, the unboxing video and clear photos of the issue.
  • Refund claims without the required unboxing video may be declined, except where applicable law requires otherwise.
  • Do not send any item back unless Velora Jewells provides written instructions.
  • If a claim is reviewed and approved, the refund method and estimated processing time will be communicated. Bank or payment-provider processing times may vary.
  • This policy does not exclude or restrict any consumer rights or remedies that cannot legally be excluded under applicable Indian law.

Payments & cash on delivery

  • Online payments are processed through the payment provider shown during checkout. Velora does not store your full card details or UPI PIN.
  • Minimum product subtotal is ₹500, excluding shipping. Shipping is currently shown as free.
  • For COD orders, the current checkout flow requires a ₹70 online advance; the remaining product subtotal is due at delivery. The advance and balance are shown before you pay.
  • An order is confirmed only after the server verifies the payment. A payment-provider success screen alone is not the final order confirmation.
  • If a payment is pending, do not pay repeatedly. Use the payment reference to contact support.

Privacy policy

To fulfil an order, the store processes the name, mobile number, optional email, delivery address, order contents and payment reference supplied at checkout. These details are used for order processing, delivery, support, fraud prevention and legally required records.

Payment card or UPI credentials are handled by the payment provider; do not send them to store support. Limited technical events, such as product views and cart actions, may be recorded to improve store operation. Access to order and customer records is restricted to authorised store administration.

Contact the official support channel to request help with personal information or an order record. Information may be retained where required for tax, accounting, fraud prevention or legal obligations.

Terms of service

  • Order confirmation depends on payment verification and stock availability. If an order cannot be fulfilled after payment, support will contact you and arrange an appropriate resolution.
  • Courier timelines are estimates and may change due to factors outside the store's control.
  • These terms are subject to applicable Indian law and mandatory consumer rights.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum order value?

The product subtotal must be at least ₹500. Shipping is currently free.

How does COD work?

Pay the ₹70 advance online at checkout. The remaining product subtotal is due to the delivery agent, as shown in your order confirmation.

When is my order confirmed?

After the payment is verified and the store successfully creates your order, you will see an order number.

What if an item arrives damaged or incorrect?

Keep the packaging, record a continuous unboxing video, and contact support promptly with your order number and evidence.

Can I cancel or change an order?

Contact support as soon as possible. Changes may not be possible after packing or dispatch.
